     Barbara turned from the window. That giant locusts were eating everything at her industrial farm was unnerving to watch. They were planting new crops per the orders of that dude with the robe and the book in their wake. It was unnerving seeing the new crops shoot up in seconds.

She didn't know she had GMO anything to warrant this. She didn't know her farm was being re-optimized away from modern industrial farming for some magically enhanced crap she wanted nothing to do with. But the GMO crops were crying or some shit. Grow, die, get fed to people. Barbara didn't CARE.

But apparently on her end nothing would change. Well yields were gonna soar. Buying more trucks would be a headache. Could she rig harness on the giant rolly pollies that were her new harvesters? Or if she was really angrily whimsical... is she getting a flying sleigh and reindeer?!

The grapevine said this was happening all over. Her life was being forcingly switched from one thing she understood and had the skills for to another thing she mostly didn't understand, but her skills perfectly transferred over for. Which she didn't believe. Even if she'd demonstrated it already. It'd break the second Harry Potter with a ?Kindle? left.

This was college all over again. And in college she buried herself in social media.

She fired up her tablet, waited till the TV bothered to let the tablet connect, and got ready to scroll.

"That shit out there is awful. Show me who I can blame," her tablet asked for more details,"Right. 'Um user? data so I can give context' There are wizards dancing giant locusts in my field eating all 'unnatural' crops."

"Here's what I could find on that. Oh Note. That wizard is a ..."

"I don't care."

"Fair enough (^_^) your context on who to blame."

There was a video of some Aggri Pundit, "Yes, your crops are being turfed out. Yes, your soil is being enhanced back to a higher standard of richness. But no this is not some divine or governmental mandate. We don't know yet who is behind this... science and magic miracle... What else do I call it. Everything you do NOW translates over. Some of that makes sense. Some of it is bizarre."

"Next article."

"On the current subject?"

"Yeah," she was getting fed up. Maybe she needed to tweak the settings again... but at least it wasn't talking about some celebrity's high tech makeover that let them get back into acting.

"Most of the other articles on the subject are rehashing this story."

Barabara screamed in frustration,"Show me all the headlines!"

She scrolled and scrolled and scrolled. Nothing. She put the tablet back to sleep exasperated. "I don't care what the rich fucks are doing to cope. And save a few NONE of them are coping! That does not distract ME from how I'M NOT COPING!! Don't tell me this shit!" She'd already turned the tablet back on mid-rant. She couldn't escape her need to be online.

The news pages had refreshed with a fresh article,"OH my days! Articles on how woe is me the poor are don't help anyone either. Fuck them. They can't buy better bread. Boo fucking hoo. The Flying feather duster are keeping you alive aren't they?!?" She calmed a sec,"At least they aren't robbing me to feed the welfare shits. My fields. My grain. My money. Period. I need a distraction. A real one," the house shook,"Fuck you damn bugs." Barbara steadied her milk in the bridge and got out her butter to warm it. Then another rumble and she told the ceiling,"Move quieter." She measured exact amounts for her watchtime snack. There was precisesly on bowl before she was out, "Reality is out to fuck me. No dinner no sweet talk. Okay."

She started her popcorn, and got ready to at least WATCH something instead of the... giant nasty beasts eating crops SHE PAID GOOD MONEY FOR. And no she didn't care that she was getting value back. Her decision was stolen from her by god damned Harry Potter!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! AAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAA!
