How do you end up a mile underground in a spherical shape hunting for crystals long since mined, or whatever, and charged by Ancestral Warriors or Scientists of yet another dead species? Practice.

A soft tone is emitted from within her spherical shape, and the tone itself is warped and molded into a glittering explosive. When the music within the explosive expires, the rock nearby in a roundish shape is atomized. Everything not destroyed looks burnt and cratered more than ever in this unused tunnel expansion. The little mine was stronger by far than the drilling tools used to bore the place out. At least if you want speed.

A single crystal carved or shaped through sound only into a rather old world, if phallic, shape. She’s found the bright red fuselage and golden exhaust flame of a… Missile Crystal… a bit retrograde. Modeled on a child's toy. To get through unsuspecting customs officials? It’s the weirdest version she’s discovered yet. No crystal casing, nothing to socket into her suit system and have it appear in readouts before coming into being and use as needed. She was used to energy and matter manipulation.

This crystal, et cætera was basically magic. Reality manipulation through Song - Not Energy. Her latest suit system ran purely on it. She found this crystal just through humming and listening for the echo. She couldn’t recall -right now- if she had perfect pitch. That worried her.

The rock was staying destroyed. This also worried her. Not just an energy pattern disruption soon to reorient. Song and notes to destroy rocks. Where was the science that was otherwise riff? What was next, creation? Internally she smirked. Next she’ll be making music to make pathways to walk or jump on. A song to hold targets in place? She was worried again. How much music would she need to go through on one single planet? Would the systems store properly for later removal?

But she was stranded. She was really sick of being stranded. For once not shot down, just out of gas to exit the energy field protecting the world. Out of gas to exit the system.

A 'Toll gate'. Put in place by whomever. Many cultures oin the fringes used them to hold tourists hostage for… proper monitoring gear to spy on them and fine them if the idyllic Gaea World was sullied in their vacation there. Others to make sure you heard a sales pitch. Some prison worlds used them so you could come and visit but had to have a MONUMENTAL level of scanning done before they refunded you and allowed you to leave. If they refunded you.

One way to keep things working. Otherwise the field would be protecting nothing by now. Well depending on any reactors and if they were online. The shield muddled signals. Perhaps intentionally. She recalled a world that the Toll Gate set in motion systems to restart everything. Great for their mechanical based society to make sure they had visitors. Trade was literally 'lifesblood'.

Was someone sealing this song crap away? She emitted a soft tone and returned to her full height. Almost too fast. Just like turning into energy… Song Energy. She dared not get hit. She feared the Song of Shape would just stop abruptly, and kill her outright. Or whatever it was called. Maybe it was just ‘Whole Note’ or ‘Rest’ or whatever. She hated remembering those elective courses. The shape was at least the same if not how she got there.

That… she was used to. She scanned the crystal. Nothing. She hummed and tried… there. Didn’t have to wait for the echo. Calibrated? Anyway, Scan Confirmed. This new suit… nameless but working… listed it as a missile system upgrade. So it should be fine… for all the new crap she was bound to be shooting at. As soon as she could load it.

She didn’t know how to use it. No menu said anything. No new menus. She’d have to pack it somehow and trudge it back to ADAM. Two minds. A solution maybe with both of them working on it.

He’d probably tell her to sing at it.

She stopped dead, poised ready to jump and climb on out. Could it be so simple? She looked way up at the silence and distance. There’d be 'time enough', to waste, for a little ‘throwing notes’ around. Stranded and without any clues as she was.

She sang a tone, Missile Crystal held in front of her. Too low? She tried higher. Nothing. No sound reached out of her suit anyway, she was uniquely frustrated. A new building sensation… since she picked it up. Mountains of frustration. It made her want to… scream. In the sea of foreign emotion she considered it calmly. Why not?

She screamed at the hand held item… obeying the planted motivation, this time.

The extra sensation cleared. Renewed Clarity, and an energy transfer from carved crystal to light and sound to... 5 missiles added. It made sense. Explosions aren’t harmonic, not like the little sphere mines. They’re angry, discordant… usually chemical-reaction-like volatility engines. So this being a solid… yell? Why Not? Music was just that weird. At least it was stored. Not floating about her like musical notes ready to be grabbed out of the air and fired more like arrows.

She counted other blessings.

She didn’t have to sing a song about a wall crumbling while dodging the weird weird life on this planet. ADAM’s suggestion. He wasn’t a music teacher.

She herself didn’t know anything about music the suit wasn’t telling her. Bar ditties from memory didn’t count. She ignored the electives again. She didn’t WANT to recall more than lyrics from the bar ditties, and they were all about sex and romance. She didn’t love anything on this rock. There was still a weight in her hand. She stared at it quizzically. Just what was going on? A second step?

The crystal, after draining, remained. An eyebrow twitched -her emotion or the suit's? She clenched her fist just a tad, a handshake pressure, and… poof! It was dust. Like she wasn’t glittery enough, it settled almost entirely on her. Nothing to show ADAM. Maybe just the scan would be enough. She’d scan the next drained crystal.

She felt like she should try something else in the relative safety of the serene moment. She hummed what had pinged the suit, and the missiles, and checked her HUD.

Harmonic Suit v2. V2? Energy Capacity… missile cap… wow… missile types… she’d normally have no idea before acquiring. She was humming longer than she ever had before, easy as pie. She hummed, she perused, she mused. For once, well twice, she knew what was missing in upgrades. Unlike the Ing thefts, she saw items she’d never heard of… and nothing was garbled.

This could work. The militaristic had no trouble with Gift Horses. The Suit's emotional sledgehammer was non-plussed and blasé. Deeper to the core of her being, she was uneasy. Grandfather would have a team looking into it… partly because it seemed a tad more alive than a tool of battle or exploration. She would stick it in a vault rather than wear it and once she had enough intel she'd put it on only on rare occasions. It made her feel … safe however. Safe from what? And why not 'protected'?

Who or what's emotion was THAT?

She focused back on field tests in the serenity of the space.

She sang up, or rather growled up, a missile, nice and fast, no switch, no rigid arm movements. If she was as mad as a pirate she could blast her own face. She went back to Chozo Standard Battle Stance. No sense dishonoring the fallen. Or misaiming.

No feeling of needing to use it instantly. Very not Chozo feeling. No racing heart. Still exhilarating, she had that same idea of destructive force. But the calm, could lead to great aiming. And the disdainful growl would fit with ADAM’s insistence she acclimate to more normal human behavior.

Monster? Little shocked growl. Annoyance? Little more disdainful.

She fired at the wall. She hadn’t stopped humming. It was too easy to keep it up. Like her inner monologue. She wondered if storing the suit away on the ship if she'd still be… musical like this. If this was a part of her now. Gift or Curse.

Like how since The Baby she'd taken a few more… protective missions. A few more,"Well in their shoes I'd be on the comm gear just as frantic." Ideas that weren't really there outside a sense of duty before. She who can, does. Anything less lets the evil, the selfish, the corrupt win the day.

'Win the day.' There was some ADAM rummaging around in her Gifts and Curses as well. She chuckled genuinely and imagined having a Gifts and Curses HUD Menu.

Something harmonized with the chuckle and cut the chuckle dead in its tracks. A sense -from deeper than the suit- of bar games. Well something translated that glimpse in her head.

The focus of the moment brought her back to base reality, and made her glance at the blast crater. A bit too big. Her hair would be flapping and on fire if not for the suit even in this atmosphere. Likely more damage yield. Just an easy flick too. It didn’t seem like it should have had a muzzle or light flash outside the blast itself. Just an ordinary missile in that regard. Was it the humming? Was The Song involved?

She sang louder. Missile two. Bigger explosion. Actual flash, more light and glittering dying particles. She checked menus. She was out? Oh the second missile was worth three. She had a submenu listing expenditures. Nice. And she had supers highlighted. Unlocked? No special item for that? Something louder from her voice box? How to charge the suit on this semi high tech rock? Jam session? Suit energy? Both? Probably both. Nothing was left super science particle wise so far.

She needed to clear her mind from some of these invasive feelings.

She meditated, and unbidden Sang a Song of the Chozo people. It came to mind, heart, and voice… well the voice part was outside normal human range… with notes she shouldn’t know… easy as her breathing would normally be. Her mind raced with images of Chozo Warriors training, fighting and dying. Chozo Scientists discovering… both success and failure. A peace treaty argued for expertly.

She felt pride and calm. She felt… from the warriors… a sense of noble defeat. From her military background in the Galactic Federation a sense of a speech in debriefing signalling a just end. And more of that calm.

She also felt invigorated! Ready for anything. And… menus indicated she was. Time had incremented. Five minutes, and done? Normally she took two minutes. Her recovery room count skyrocketed on her suit map of discovered areas. Missiles and bombs sprouted as well. More like a map ping that wouldn’t last -no calibration- but she recalled where perfectly. The recall was a pure suit augmentation. Like her new vocal range.

A super bomb was ROUGHLY nearby. While that would be nice... what's the arming on one of those?

In the heat of battle, there’d be times when humming/shouting/anything but shooting would be too distracting, infeasible, and she suspected some critters would react negatively. Even reacting friendly could be bad. Metroids could be lovely, even sweet, said something in her core… it felt like Grandfather. Just they were so ravenous you’re better off not befriending them without something to point them at. Even a full reactor, might only be a distraction before coming distractingly close or in the way. A baby though… she switched back to adults and variants quickly.

Like Phazon Metroids… they were fresh and new… she focused her mind.

She imagined something new yanking energy… maybe yanking music out of her. She recalled the dust. The kinda complete death a Metroid could cause. Was that like a Metroid draining something of its… music as well as its lifeforce?

Chilled, she resolved that she needed off this rock. It was too dangerous. Hadn’t even met anything terrible yet. She just… knew? Oddly from multiple internal perspectives once again. Like that deep deep feeling. Primal. It shored up her desire to exfiltrate. Paired with it. Like travelling together.

In her calm focus she concentrated harder. The Suit was bridging SOMETHING deep to something more recent through the alien lens of all this music crap. She had a sudden recall of Chozo Scientists tinkering with the first Metroids. A leap. The pairing was Metroids and Scientists hunting for X or something and wanting to get out of dodge. It was unsafe to remain. So they should leave. Together. Her military training resolved that was enough to answer that for a minute and everything seemed to accept the resolution. She was in control. She was the final arbiter. But it still worried her that it was otherwise all in there still firing around muddling her calm measured logic.

She worried alone in the near dark. Humming helped her thoughts now. Back to ADAM on the chance the sensors picked up something for the toll gate. Before everything popped open. A real risk with a tolled world. Everything seemed powered for months, maybe more, but something might feed on it. Might be feeding on it. Maybe her apprehension was the suit reading something from the planet. Some quizzical disharmony she couldn’t hear but it felt.

Might explain some of the musical gibberish devices missing. Half science half music. Maybe something would refine the sensation. Maybe the world was hurting like … wow too many she’d been to.

ADAM must have a clue. Up and out. She was not gonna think about leviathans right now. She’d given Phazon a passing thought already today. It didn't need any more.

Eensy Weensy Spider did not give and activate spider ball for the record. It was listed as an eventual, but not apparently a freebie. She’d need ADAM’s help for any other dumb song tricks to try. He timed headshots on callouts to the ditty. Hot cross buns for clambering down rocks, and not just jumping off the cliff and landing.

There wasn't a ditty about jumping UP surfaces coming to mind.

The long, jumpy, way up was her life story, and this world would continue it. She left meditating and focus training and resumed her traipsing about the local compounds. The arm cannon was at least normal enough and good for blasting this and that as usual. She did find she tended to fire warning shots a touch more, and it WORKED HERE. And not just on flora and pseudo-flora.

Made some sense here… okay it made sense most of the time for destructive powers and devices. If it was really a Chozo Trial, she’d have had to do things faster, and would have been led to a meditation chamber before here entirely. This was serendipity itself recharging on demand.

Like hearing Pirate clomping and chittering carrying something important enough for slow reverence. Or fear. You just know to investigate. Serendipity.

The combat piece was a Chozo combat song she’d never heard. In her spliced DNA? The suit translated the knowledge of where to fire into a still image of her target and when it lined up blammo? If she was a little something more she might have a better explanation. Still, it felt right. No camera at barrel tip so must have been a s… no. It was drooling. Fresh.

Harmonically… err sonar shaping… but color? She admired her barrel's tip. Nothing there. Combat sense covered some data, harmonics and maybe sonar covered more. Questions. So much would remain unanswered.

Later she thought sweeping the body in readiness. She wasn't taking chances in case something sprouted from the body. Either body. She scanned the new corpse. ‘Wendigo’? Terran folklore. It had a name now. Wendigo made cannibalism suggestions. One path herbaceous one path carnivorous. It would explain similarities. But not the loss of rolling in one and the … later.

She checked the door. Closed?

She could annihilate the corpses before cannibalism. Yes. The thought felt right.

Roll. Sorrow. Detonation, timing. Super Bomb Renewal achieved. No corpses. Some invigoration. Some serious recharge of containment and other systems. 15 missiles. She could work with these.

Door unlocked.

In the calm she reviewed the battle. She could have lassoed the horn and flung it, sparing herself some emotional pain. Wendigo was dead. This one anyway. Likely more. Almost always were. More practice to get some aspects right. Her cannon was her thing. Not swinging around animal bits.

Next time.

The children and younger smaller adults trained down the tubes, but larger adults didn’t arrive. Stuck? There was a tremor. A second. A crash after a pause and… mom was in the arena. She’d opened an upper room? Oh the ceiling door.

Mom set up in the arena center. She listened intently to something. Oh. She was blind. She… regurgitated… liquid rock and shaped up a nest in seconds. Other adults came to shore up the lip of the nest.

A child rolled right to Samus. She knelt down and petted the … scan … three day old. The young one looked to their left and Samus instinctively looked and aimed. She felt nothing but semi belligerent dociles so what was here?

Nothing? It pushed harder into her hand. A friend-or-foe check? On day three? It was that smart. They all were. The tension left the herd. This was a meditation site. They instinctively found old training paths, the youngest rolled in tubes unable to get very far climbing or rolling before resting. Meditation, nesting, and a creche. A herbivore trifecta.

Gave her plenty of time to check the Selection Chamber again as the tube was the easy way back up… since she had no Screw Attack. The ceiling looked robust enough to land a small frigate, so no drop off from the ship either.

The chamber was untouched… no. There was ceiling settling. She checked both chambers before dropping down. Selection was basically blocked off. You could roll into the room and back down to the arena floor. Ahem. Primary Cyclops Paddock. The viewer had detached and blocked the train from accidentally ending up in the Selection chamber again. Still worked fine. Mom’s leap had seismically thrown the dead viewer in Assessment against the lockers hiding the kill beam with ease. Upside down it showed the view above the P.C.Paddock. The view panned easily, cameras vibrant if ignored for centuries. She rotated the viewer and took in the vista. An office? And a now wide open walkway. Mom’s exit point.

Coast clear, she descended to the alpha predator chamber. The fake paths up now clattered underfoot. Just chrome, corrosion, and useless equipment. Nothing had moved in here. The wall sealed, and the airlock shield reformed.

There was a combative tremor.

What now? She opened the door. Two cyclops were cleaning the house sometimes with each other bodily. Consume rock, spit it elsewhere. They made a big show of charging. No leap so they were not trying to hurt Samus. They still hit like a boulder. Angry youths? She lasso-redirected and left ceilingward. They clonked into each other and resumed setting up their ‘deadly’ game of bumper cars. Energy held her in place as the floor shield sealed. The office owner could walk right over and look in, take notes, and head back to their desk.

So the cyclops kept up Predator vs Prey combat trials, for an observer long gone. Fine tradition to have built into your local DNA. The walkway was otherwise obscured from Toll Gate view. Kept whoever, 'less' shot at, she supposed. She could land the ship on the PCP, and jump over to enter here whenever she wanted. She added it to her map herself. Landing Copernicus.

She crossed to the office, easily ignoring the open air view. Something hit her in the back. She turned and aimed. The camera?

Something lived on it. She carefully dodged and scanned. ‘Turret Sprite’ reproduces by lodging eggs in tough hide. Sings to self fertilize. Handy. She caught the next shot.

Turret Missile.

Okay… why…? For deaf targets to get shot in the posterior? She answered herself. She climbed onto the walkway roof and ran over to pop the Turret Sprite off the camera.

She had to bounce her shots off the glass of the walkway. It had friends who were likely to destroy her suit's containment seal. Dodge fire, dodge fire… dodge almost into a face full of pellets, fire.

Then she had to Turret the turrets. She controlled when the three potent shots fired. One shot per sprite. She was empty of missiles, but also targets.

So, she reflected, some purely opportunistic life still lived. Good to know? She broke off the growth on her back, and swung down to the walkway.

She popped the airlock to the office and passed inside. Once through the second airlock she prepared to scan everything.

Dry logs of proto missile -horn- output and transfer logs to Primary Harmonics: Missile Shaping… and where that was. She’d travel through the other door in the Rowdy Youth/Secondary Cyclops Paddock. Indications were that the receiving system was gone. Oh.

Chance PH:MS was empty to the walls? 90+

She was just curious enough to look but not memorize how it happened. There was no telling on this planet. Was it exploitive or scientific? Or barbaric?

She hoped for science.

She dodged the rambunctious young cyclopses, and kept them out of the doorway. PH:MS… and PH had no remaining equipment. But they did have scannable data screens and terminals. All of this was encrypted. But she could grab all of it anyway. One. Only one was decrypted. A video… another science milestone logged for posterity.

A siren-like amphibious life form sang to a scientist, and in the process of serenading them a rock nearby changed shape. The swooning scientist was kept from delivering the crystal to the siren, and was evacuated. They passed signs and readouts, and the lovelorn was behind closed doors. The crystal was instead touted in the unknown language, and compared to a harvested horn. A machine transferred the energy from one crystal to the other now in a … mold.

One Missile Crystal. Quite the original price. Likely a first successful paramour extraction. Others she suspected died. Consumed by the Siren. No idea from her first viewing. ADAM would be a better eye for their -likely very lost- language on the readouts and screens.

Decryption would require security. Wherever it was. Where all it was. Could be several locations for snippets, or security unlocks. Or one lump somewhere. PH was after all large enough to be a major site for their prepackaging division. Now little more than a hangar.

A hangar. ADAM could fly in here if she was indisposed. Once the roof was unlocked. Always something. But contingencies for future problems were always good to have waiting in the wings.

She dodged the bumper cars and headed up again. She climbed onto the walkway. She guesstimated the hangar roofline. The foliage along it didn’t move. Perfect camouflage otherwise. Also the lack of age and adaptation. Good enough otherwise. Space borne minds would miss it. Local Life knew what was or was not natural. Besides the native long gone population, she might be one of the only ones well space traveled who’d notice.

Pirates would fly right past it. Looks like planetary defenses. Don’t poke the nest.

She took in the more distant views. There was that dish, and its building. Other side of the lip of this plateau. It looked like something did live on the side of that building. Up from below then. Water far distant but not like on the horizon. Behind her more plateaus, and jungle until the jungle petered out. Plateaus ground down to a desert maybe. Deeper jungle beyond the hangar and mountains. Beyond the landing site Beneath the Iris… looked like plains in the distance. Something glinted. The rest of the world? Would take an aerial overlook. Serious mapping.

But this helped. Relative safety while doing this also helped. She ducked into the office to meditate.

The dish might well be next. Map suggested it was up from that door in the PCP. No hint as to the area beneath it. Could be anything. Still plenty of space on this plateau, and of course likely spaces in the planetary crust. Didn’t seem, for now, to be any structures hovering in the sky. If there were, hopefully they would not have aerial rail security systems between platforms. Hopefully her near future had no cannons launching her across the planet.

Landings hurt.

She made her way back to ADAM. Two minds could make more sense of the growing exploratory puzzle before she traveled through the former arena door. Then on to the little known.

And beyond.
